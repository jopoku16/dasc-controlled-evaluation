LEAD 2 (phase-blindness kill test): analysis protocol, written 2026-11-02 BEFORE any tuning or evaluation run of the new variants.
The ALIVE / KILLED thresholds are fixed in the study plan; everything below is the operationalisation chosen
before the runs. Nothing here was changed after seeing evaluation results.

CELLS: electricity and weather x naive24 and ridge (4 cells). Projected (clipped) updates only. Target 0.90, alpha = 0.10.
BASELINES (reused by re-running their stored configurations; determinism is checked against the stored summaries):
  ACI projected   = main-run chosen configuration (results/tuning/<dataset>_<forecaster>_chosen.csv, method aci_clip), 12 configurations
  DASC tuned      = results/tuning/<dataset>_<forecaster>_dascgamma_chosen.csv, variant dasc_clip, 60 configurations

HOUR-OF-DAY MAD (primary): mean over the 24 clock hours of |coverage_h - 0.90| on the evaluation steps (t >= dev_end), unweighted,
  exactly the hour-of-day coverage deviation computed in the main study. Clock hour = hour of the true timestamp.
  Secondary: mean absolute deviation of hourly coverage from its own mean (reported, not used in the verdict).

NEW VARIANTS (module code/lead2_variants.py; dasc_lib.py is not edited):
  Kernel weight for pool member i at time t:  exp(-(d_mag^2 + w^2 * c^2) / h^2)
    d_mag = l2 distance of the 6-band magnitude features (unchanged DASC feature, window fw)
    c     = chord distance between unit vectors (cos a, sin a): 2 |sin((a_t - a_i)/2)|
    phase variant:    a_t = arg of the daily-frequency DFT coefficient of y[t-m..t-1] (m = min(t, fw); bin fw/24 when m = fw), mean removed
                      (extrapolated to forecast time t; the advance is a constant rotation for full windows and cancels in c)
    calendar variant: a_t = 2*pi*hour(t)/24, hour from the timestamp (deterministic, allowed)
  Drift score D_t, the gate and everything else are the unchanged DASC definitions and use ONLY the six magnitude columns
  (the 64-step mean of cos/sin is not meaningful because 64 is not a multiple of 24). ACI update projected on [0.01, 0.35].
  w = 0 reproduces DASC exactly (checked bit for bit).

TUNING (identical grids and identical rules for the phase and the calendar variant; same development prefix 0 <= t < 0.25 n,
  same criterion: mean interval score at level 0.10 on the development steps; ties broken by earlier configuration, as in tuning.py):
  stage 1 (48): pool x h x lambda x m_min_frac x fw  = {2 pools} x {0.35,0.55,0.80} x {0.15,0.45} x {0.25,0.5} x {168,336}, gamma = 0.015, w = 1
  stage 2 (6):  gamma in {0.005,0.01,0.015,0.02,0.03,0.05} at the stage-1 winner
  stage 3 (12): h in {0.35,0.55,0.80} x w in {0.5,1,2,4} at the stage-2 winner's gamma and the stage-1 structure
  Total 66 configurations (repeats counted, as DASC tuned counts 48 + 6 + 6 = 60). Budget for ACI projected is 12, for DASC tuned 60.
  Hindsight (not a selection rule, not used in the verdict): for each variant and cell the evaluation-period MAD of every one of the 66
  configurations is stored, so the best attainable MAD in the family can be reported as an upper bound on what any tuning could do.

PAIRED INFERENCE: moving-block bootstrap, block 168, 2000 resamples, np.random.default_rng(0), blocks drawn with
  metrics._block_indices(n, 168, rng) in the same sequence as metrics.paired_block_bootstrap (n = number of evaluation steps),
  so the blocks are those of results/real_paired_bootstrap_dascgamma.csv. In that one loop the width, coverage, score and the
  per-resample hour-coverage MAD of every method are computed. Percentile 95% intervals.

PER-CELL TESTS (point estimates unless said; the interval is reported next to each):
  T1  MAD reduction against ACI projected >= 30%  [(MAD_ACI - MAD_new) / MAD_ACI]
  T2  MAD reduction against DASC tuned >= 30%
  T3  marginal coverage of the new variant within 0.01 of 0.90
  T4  relative width difference against ACI projected AND against DASC tuned <= +1% (point estimate; the upper 95% bound is also reported)
  T5  not matched by calendar: the paired interval of (MAD_phase - MAD_calendar) lies entirely below zero.
      Matched = interval includes zero, or phase worse than calendar.
CELL STATUS: ALIVE if T1..T5 all hold. WEAK-KILL if phase MAD reduction (against either baseline) is below 10%, or phase is matched by calendar (T5 fails).
VERDICT: ALIVE only if all four cells are ALIVE. KILLED if the reduction is below 10% or phase is matched by calendar in any of the four cells.
  (The study plan says KILLED if the reduction is below 10% or equals the calendar-hour kernel's; the strict any-cell reading is adopted; the
  per-cell table is shown so a reader can apply a looser reading.) Anything else: INCONCLUSIVE.

CAVEAT FIXED IN ADVANCE: with about 375 (electricity) or 750 (weather) evaluation steps per hour, even a perfectly conditionally
  calibrated interval has a nonzero MAD from sampling noise. The noise floor is estimated and reported; if a 30% reduction is
  impossible in a cell because of the floor, that is reported, the criterion is NOT changed, and the verdict is shown with and without that cell.

INVARIANCE / PREDICTION OF HOUR (Task 1): pairs (t, i), t over the evaluation steps, i drawn uniformly from the 1440 steps before t
  (30 partners per anchor, seed 0). Spearman correlation of feature distance with circular hour difference min(|dh|, 24-|dh|);
  95% interval by resampling whole days (blocks of 24 consecutive anchor steps) with the global ranks held fixed.
